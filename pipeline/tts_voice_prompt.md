# Gemini TTS – Hindi Narrator Voice Prompt

Use these in Gemini TTS for the Devanagari storytelling narration.

## Voice description

Deep, warm Indian male baritone with crisp, clear Devanagari pronunciation. Rich chest resonance, close-mic intimacy, and high studio loudness.

## Style

Expressive, emotionally gripping storytelling at a steady, engaging pace. Read continuous, unpunctuated Devanagari text smoothly, with only brief, natural micro-pauses between complete thoughts. No long or frequent pauses. Stress key emotions and impactful words with stronger projection, chest weight, and dynamic pitch shifts, without slowing down or dragging.
